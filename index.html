Statistical instability of a stochastically forced shear

Overview·Digested·Raw

Overview

For a fixed elongated rectangular torus and forcing in the single vorticity mode \(\sin y\), the stochastically forced two-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations have, at sufficiently small viscosity, an ergodic invariant measure not supported on shear flows.
