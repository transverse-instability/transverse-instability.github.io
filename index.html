Statistical instability of a stochastically forced shear

Overview·Digested·Raw·Lean

Overview

For a fixed elongated rectangular torus and forcing in the single vorticity mode \(\sin y\), the stochastically forced two-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations have, at sufficiently small viscosity, an ergodic invariant measure not supported on shear flows.

  • Digested: a readable account of the argument.
  • Raw: the full AI-written proof.
  • Lean: formal verification in Lean.

Workflow

The starting point is a raw proof, written by an AI model and verified in Lean. The raw proof is then digested into an account written for human readers.

We then work on the project in a Git repository, either by adding to or changing the raw file, or by digesting material that has already been written in raw form.

This is an open, ongoing project. It is open to broader collaboration, and its goal is the eventual release of a single article.

Pros

  • The developments are time-stamped and saved.
  • Results are open and accessible to everybody.
  • The focus is on digesting proofs rather than on producing them.
